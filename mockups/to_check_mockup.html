How it would work

  • The invoice check makes a list, you decide. Each row the check could not settle becomes an item on the To check tab. The Budget tab shows the count and puts a chip on every line that has an item.
  • Every item says what is odd in one sentence and offers the answers that fit that kind of problem. Each button shows what it will change before you tap it.
  • Resolve = decide. Save = apply. Picking an answer only stages it (“Resolved — not saved”). Save on the card, or Save all in the bar, applies it to the budget and marks the row Resolved in the backup sheet’s To check tab with the note, name and date. Undo on the card or Discard all throws staged answers away.
  • Reopen is staged too. Saving a reopen puts the money back and sets the backup row to Open; the old answer stays in the card’s History.
  • Leaving with unsaved answers (another tab, refresh, close) asks “Save your resolutions?”. Try it with Simulate refresh or any tab.
  • One tap resolves. Answers that need a choice (another line, a split, a new line) open a picker that shows the effect live and saves with one more tap.
  • Nothing is final. Undo right after, or Reopen any time from Resolved. Reopening puts the money back where it was.
  • Who and when are saved with every answer, plus an optional note.
  • Not sure? “Not sure yet” hides it for a week. “Ask someone” sends the question and parks it under Waiting.
  • Select several of the same kind to answer them together (for example both Star Interiors rows, or all four with no category).
  • Projected cost per line = budget or invoices, whichever is higher (Done lines use invoices). That is why moving money can change it.
